Tested
On this firmware in the simulation: unit tests, 83 system tests, panel check, KPI campaign and the 8 h simulated soak. Every command ran exactly once and reached the Slave within 5 s; the remaining red cells are explained in docs/test/results.md. On real units: hardware suite 17 of 17, pair with seven power cuts (182 of 182 commands exactly once), pairing all pass, stress with 13 phones (2907 of 2907, no restart), a 1 h soak with two units and seven power cuts (1092 of 1092, game kept 7 of 7, no reset) and a 4 h soak with one unit (4797 of 4800 confirmed, none twice or lost, no restart). In that 4 h soak the test Mac lost TCP to the Master for about 17 s once while the Master kept running; the cause is open.
Not checked on this firmware: the panels and the QR code by eye, including the blinking, and the Bluetooth speaker. The owner accepted both for this release.
Known limits7
Not FIBA approved: no tenths of a second, no link to a game clock, one display side, digits of about 11 cm.
The units have no speaker. The signal comes from the operator's phone, in a hall through a Bluetooth speaker. Set the phone's screen timeout: a locked phone gives no signal.
