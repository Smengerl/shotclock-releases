Known limits3
A unit that restarts during a match while the clock stands in READY waits 10 s in its role before an auto update; if the game is taken back later than that, it may update once more.
A power cut during the 60 s trial rolls back and skips that version for the auto update (Aktualisieren still offers it).
The connect QR code does not scan on the first try in every case; the diffuser of hardware 2.0 is expected to help.
