A beta channel for engineering mode, new release notes pages and fixes in the web UI. Works with v2.0.0 and later units (same MAJOR version); units on 2.1.1 and older take this release over USB.
Highlights4
Beta channelWith engineering mode on, a unit offers the last build of main as a second update, tagged BETA, and the official release as the way back. The splash shows the version and, for a beta, a BETA label next to ENG. A club unit never sees a beta.

Known limits3
Units on v2.1.1 and older read the old release manifest, which stays as it was published: they see nothing newer than 2.1.1, are updated over USB, and do not see the notes of new versions.
Official images are not signed (#69 is not planned): a unit trusts the TLS connection to one host and the SHA-256 of the image.
The connect QR code does not scan on the first try in every case.
