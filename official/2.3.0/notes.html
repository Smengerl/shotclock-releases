#202
For people who build, the bench: a unit comes into service without a phone, python3 -m ship factory --wifi FILE --eng writes its WiFi and engineering mode over USB, and the dev command nvs_erase can keep WiFi, engineering mode and the bench host. The bench finds its units without fixed addresses, and the hardware stages pause the bench server themselves. ship factory --backup and --erase for a real unit.

Known limits4
Units on v2.1.1 and older read the old release manifest, which stays as it was published: they see nothing newer than 2.1.1, are updated over USB, and do not see the notes of new versions.
Official images are not signed (#69 is not planned): a unit trusts the TLS connection to one host and the SHA-256 of the image.
The connect QR code does not scan on the first try in every case.
