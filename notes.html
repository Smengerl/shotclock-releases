Shotclock release notes

Official versions, newest first. Units of the same MAJOR version work together.

v2.1.0 (2026-11-10)

Works with v2.0.0 units (same MAJOR version). From this release on, updates come from the public release site https://smengerl.github.io/shotclock-releases/ (#64).

Highlights

  • Update automatically (a switch per unit, off by default): at power-on the unit checks once and installs a newer version of the same MAJOR by itself, until *Spiel starten* is pressed, which stops a running download at once (#127). A version that fails its check or rolls back is not installed by itself again.
  • Guide in the web UI: the chapters *Spiel*, *Einstellungen* and *Update* on the lock screen, as coach marks on demo states; nothing is sent while it is open (#128).
  • Release notes one tap away: a link symbol after the current and the offered version opens its notes on the public site; a beta, development or side-loaded build has none (#74).
  • Public release site with the image, the update manifest, the release notes and the written offer for the LGPL libraries (#64, #59).

Fixes and changes

  • *Bedienung sperren* and *Spiel starten* switch the panel at once, without the iris, so the phone follows at once.
  • The auto-rotation turns the picture the right way after a quarter turn of the enclosure (#112).
  • A cancelled update shows no error on the panel; a rename reaches the header at once; no *Nicht verbunden* banner after the guide; corrected guide texts.
  • The status LED and the NeoPixel library are gone (#139).

Known limits

  • A unit that restarts during a match while the clock stands in *READY* waits 10 s in its role before an auto update; if the game is taken back later than that, it may update once more.
  • A power cut during the 60 s trial rolls back and skips that version for the auto update (*Aktualisieren* still offers it).
  • The connect QR code does not scan on the first try in every case; the diffuser of hardware 2.0 is expected to help (#28).

Hardware verification

On two units of hardware 1.x (2.0.0-22-gc845b1f, the code of this release): the hardware suite (19 passed), the phone stress and the 2.5 h soak with seven power cuts (commands confirmed 100 %, exactly once, game kept over every cut, no unplanned restart, no split brain). By hand: auto update with trial and skip, cancel by *Spiel starten*, power cut and WiFi loss during a download, rotation, portal, popup, guide, palette and QR code. The TCP gap of #57 did not show again (longest poll gap 6 s, only during a power cut).

v2.0.0 (2026-11-09)

A new MAJOR version (#76): V2 units do not work with v1.0.0. Every unit is moved over USB once by the owner (new partition table and the unit record, docs/users/owner.md); after that, updates come over WiFi.

Highlights

  • One image for every unit: colour, mount orientation and the club logo are put on the unit at delivery (#61, #55, #66); the id comes from the MAC, so host and hotspot names change once.
  • Update over WiFi from the Einstellungen, one unit at a time: check, download (the slot is erased first, #96), a trial and an automatic return to the old version (#22); the stock bootloader rolls back an image that fails even before it starts (#88).
  • Standby with the club logo while no game runs, locked and released from the phone with an iris on the panel (#81, #121); language switched at runtime for all units (#60).
  • New phone UI: the units in the header, the Einstellungen popup, one orange accent (#65, #77, #121).
  • Engineering mode per unit for the bench: trace over WiFi, snapshots, crash dumps (#58, #89).
  • Units of another MAJOR version are refused instead of mixing (#63).

Fixes since v2.0.0-rc.1

  • *Aus* from Ready or Pause no longer leaves the time on the panel; a switched-off shot clock shows no number (FIBA Art. 50, #121).
  • The panel turns only on agreeing accelerometer readings and retries a sensor that did not answer after a restart, so the picture no longer stays upside down after an update (#112).
  • Every drawing is turned and gamma-corrected in one place (#114); the update ring shows the part still to come in the unit's dimmed colour (#122).
  • Popup: the arrow, the shadow and the spacing; a shown code and the sign-out question end with the popup; renaming keeps the phone's keyboard (#121).

Known limits

  • No public update site yet: units update from the private repository; the release channel (#64) and the LGPL question (#59) follow in V3.
  • The connect QR code on the real panel scans only sporadically (#2).
  • A slight flicker of the panel during the download remains (#96, accepted).
  • Open hardware and by-eye checks of #98 are accepted for this release, not run.

Hardware verification

Both V2 units (blue Master, red Slave) on 2.0.0-rc.1-4-g51d0787, firmware identical to v2.0.0, on 2026-11-09: hardware suite 19 passed and phone stress passed (S4); a 2.5 h soak with up to 3 phones without an unplanned restart or panic, every command exactly once (S5); 7 power cuts over WiFi with the game kept in all 7 and pairing back in 6–8 s (#124); every screen and the QR code checked by eye (S6).

v1.0.0 (2026-11-03)

First official release, after the renovation of the architecture. Units of this MAJOR version do not work with v0.1.0-rc.1: flash both units with the same release. Compared with v1.0.0-rc.1 it fixes two faults found on real units.

Highlights

  • Clean architecture: core, view, net, platform and app layers, documented designs and contracts, rules enforced in CI.
  • Pairing on both units: the Slave shows the Master's code and QR code too (#26). Commands need a paired phone (#3).
  • Clock face: new digits (Exo 2 Black Italic, 39 px), no status text on the panel. While stopped the time stays on the panel and the ring blinks, as FIBA Article 50 asks (#30).
  • Correction: + 1 s also works after the time ran out by mistake.
  • Robustness: a Slave whose Master disappears counts on and expires by itself (#19), a command storm stays below the limits (#14), a font problem stops the build (#15).
  • Licences: page /licenses in the web UI, linked in the footer (#29).
  • Setup: portal in German and English (#4, #12), new WiFi without reflashing (#5).

Fixes since v1.0.0-rc.1

  • The phone no longer loses a Slave's name, colour and firmware during a game (#42).
  • A client that stops reading, or connects to the broker and stays silent, no longer stalls the Master for seconds (#47).

Tested

On this firmware in the simulation: unit tests, 83 system tests, panel check, KPI campaign and the 8 h simulated soak. Every command ran exactly once and reached the Slave within 5 s; the remaining red cells are explained in docs/test/results.md. On real units: hardware suite 17 of 17, pair with seven power cuts (182 of 182 commands exactly once), pairing all pass, stress with 13 phones (2907 of 2907, no restart), a 1 h soak with two units and seven power cuts (1092 of 1092, game kept 7 of 7, no reset) and a 4 h soak with one unit (4797 of 4800 confirmed, none twice or lost, no restart). In that 4 h soak the test Mac lost TCP to the Master for about 17 s once while the Master kept running; the cause is open.

Not checked on this firmware: the panels and the QR code by eye, including the blinking, and the Bluetooth speaker. The owner accepted both for this release.

Known limits

  • Not FIBA approved: no tenths of a second, no link to a game clock, one display side, digits of about 11 cm.
  • The units have no speaker. The signal comes from the operator's phone, in a hall through a Bluetooth speaker. Set the phone's screen timeout: a locked phone gives no signal (#34).
  • After a Master power cut the other unit takes over after about 23 s; the clock counts on, the phone pairs again (#18).
  • Pairing keeps bystanders out, not an attacker on the same WiFi (#21). The code travels over MQTT in clear text.
  • The QR codes scan only from some distance (#2). The USB port on the back does not work yet (#27).
  • A unit mounted in another orientation needs its own build (#24).
  • There is no update over WiFi; the firmware is flashed over USB (#22).

Files

shotclock-<colour>-<lang>-1.0.0.bin: red or blue for the unit, de or en for the language of panel, web UI and portal. SHA256SUMS holds the checksums. The UI of this release is described in docs/design/target-ui.md of the tag.

For at least three years after you received this firmware, the owner provides on written request, for no more than the cost of providing it, the object files of the application, the link script and a recipe, so that you can relink it with a changed version of these libraries and install it on your unit (RELINK.md).
